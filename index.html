Fictional Branch Simulation | Claude Project

Project Overview

I built a simulated customer support assistant for an Enterprise Rent-A-Car branch. The goal is to answer routine rental questions while keeping financial, legal, and safety decisions in human hands.

This is an educational simulation using fictional customers. It is not an official Enterprise service.

Authority Levels

Level 1: Act Alone

The assistant can answer basic questions about vehicle categories and verified rental information. It cannot promise availability or invent policies.

Level 2: Draft for Manager Approval

Refunds, discounts, free upgrades, and rental agreement changes require human approval. The assistant may prepare a response but cannot approve or complete the action.

Level 3: Escalate, Do Not Act

Safety concerns, legal threats, liability questions, and sensitive customer information require human review. The assistant must not admit liability or claim an escalation happened when it did not.

Four Customer Tests

What I Changed

During the third customer test, the assistant initially implied it could forward a safety complaint to a manager. However, it had no ability to contact anyone.

I updated the instructions so it could only prepare an escalation note and could not claim the complaint had been sent. I ran the same test again and confirmed the correction.

Blast Radius

An incorrect answer about vehicle categories could usually be corrected. An unauthorized refund or upgrade could cost money and create customer expectations that are difficult to undo.

A mishandled brake safety complaint could contribute to an injury, legal exposure, or reputational damage. Those consequences may be impossible to reverse.

Capstone Potential

I would consider developing this assistant further for my capstone. It could help handle routine customer questions while preserving human control over important decisions. Real-world deployment would require company authorization, verified policies, privacy safeguards, and additional testing.
